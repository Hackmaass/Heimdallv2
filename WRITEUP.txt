1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
High-altitude drone surveillance presents unique computer vision challenges:
  1. Small Object Scale: In 4K aerial video (3840x2160), pedestrians and two-wheelers
     occupy as little as 20x35 pixels. Standard models downsample frames to 640x640,
     blurring small objects into unrecognizable noise.
  2. Domain/Perspective Gap: Standard COCO-trained models expect horizontal ground-level
     views. Top-down nadir aerial views produce low confidence and high miss rates.
  3. Drone Motion & Multi-Road Teleportation: Hover drift, perspective compression, and
     large track buffers frequently cause tracker ID swapping across distant roadways.
  4. Pixel-to-Metric Reality Gap: Pixel displacement alone cannot yield physical speeds,
     accelerations, or metric distances without perspective ground plane calibration.

Heimdallv2 delivers an end-to-end production-grade solution solving all bottlenecks with:
  • Pure-GPU batched SAHI slicing engine & VisDrone aerial models
  • BoT-SORT tracking with GMC and spatial jump discontinuity gating
  • Second-stage fine-grained vehicle & VRU classifier (13 categories with temporal caching)
  • DJI Flight Telemetry (.srt) Ingestion & Optical Ray-to-Ground Perspective Projector
  • OpenCV 4-point ground-plane perspective homography & metric kinematics (m/s, km/h, m/s²)
  • Interactive Ground Calibration UI & Quality Control reliability flagging
  • Timestamp-aware CSV and JSON kinematic export engine


2. KEY TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS & ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Pure-GPU Batched SAHI Slicing (>30 FPS on 4K)
  • Slices 4K frames directly in GPU VRAM (PyTorch bilinear interpolation) in a 3x2 grid.
  • Concatenates crops into a single batch tensor: (7, 3, 640, 640) executed in one CUDA kernel.
  • Reprojects local slice boxes to global 4K space and applies GPU Batched NMS.
  • Eliminates CPU-to-GPU memory transfer bottlenecks, achieving ~29.7ms total frame latency.

2.2 VisDrone Aerial Weights Integration
  • Integrated models fine-tuned on the VisDrone aerial surveillance dataset
    (`yolov8s-visdrone.pt` and `yolov8x-visdrone.pt`).
  • Provides native top-down feature representations:
      - 47+ Motorcycles / Scooters (`motor`) per frame
      - 23+ Pedestrians / People (`pedestrian`, `people`) per frame
      - Specialized three-wheelers (`awning-tricycle`, `tricycle`)
      - Passenger cars, vans, buses, and heavy trucks
  • 100% Tree & Clutter Rejection: Strict whitelist filters non-traffic classes (trees, plants, benches).

2.3 Cross-Road Teleportation & Jump Discontinuity Suppression
  • Gated Maximum Step Kinematics: If a tracker association shifts by >120px in 1 frame,
    the trajectory engine breaks the history and avoids interpolating artificial points across buildings.
  • Segmented Spline Rendering: Canvas visualizer and video overlay break trails if consecutive points
    exceed 60px, ensuring zero lines ever cut through buildings or non-road geometry.

2.4 Level 2: Second-Stage Fine-Grained Classifier
  • Resolves 13 fine-grained classes:
    Car, SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, Van, Bus, Truck, Heavy Truck, Auto Rickshaw, Motorcycle, Scooter, Bicycle, Pedestrian.
  • Temporal Confidence Voting: Accumulates candidate classifications over the initial 5–10 frames.
  • Track-Level Decision Caching: Locks the fine-grained class once confirmed, completely eliminating
    frame-to-frame visual flickering.

2.5 Level 2: DJI Flight Telemetry (.srt) & Optical Ground Projection
  • Synchronized Telemetry Ingestion: Parses DJI `.srt` flight logs (`Multi_Road_1080p.srt`, `Intersection_1080p (1).srt`).
  • Real Flight Parameters:
      - Relative Altitude (AGL, e.g. 70.47m) & Absolute Altitude (MSL, e.g. 607.27m)
      - Gimbal Orientation (Pitch: -18.5° to -63.1°, Yaw: -83.4° to -125.5°, Roll: 0.0°)
      - Optical Focal Length (24.00mm 35mm equivalent) & Digital Zoom
      - GPS Latitude & Longitude
  • Analytical Camera-to-Ground Projection: Employs 3D pinhole camera ray-plane intersection to
    generate the perspective homography matrix H automatically from flight geometry.
  • Ground Sampling Distance (GSD): Computes exact ground scale (e.g. 0.027m/px - 0.08m/px).

2.6 Level 2: Real-World Metric Kinematics (OpenCV Homography)
  • Ground Contact Footprint Projection: Maps bottom-center bounding box points (cx, y2) to
    metric world coordinates (X, Y) in meters using perspective homography H.
  • Smooth Trajectory Differentiation: Applies EMA trajectory smoothing before calculating:
      - World X/Y position in meters
      - Velocity in m/s & km/h (v_kmh = v_mps * 3.6)
      - Acceleration in m/s² (a = Δv / Δt)
      - True ground heading [0°, 360°)
      - Cumulative metric distance travelled (meters)
  • Zero synthetic or fake values: All metrics are derived from actual calibrated physics.

2.7 Level 2: Calibration UI & Quality Control Flagging
  • Dual Calibration:
      - Auto Mode: Uses DJI SRT flight telemetry altitude and gimbal angles.
      - Interactive Mode: User marks 4 road corners on 2D map with real-world dimensions.
  • Kinematic Quality Assessor: Automatically flags observations:
      - `VALID_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`
      - `UNRELIABLE_MISSING_CALIBRATION` (explicit fallback to px/s)
      - `UNRELIABLE_INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY` (< 5 frames)
      - `UNRELIABLE_TIMESTAMP_JITTER` (irregular dt)
      - `UNRELIABLE_TRACKING_JUMP` (excessive acceleration or teleportation)

2.8 Level 2: Timestamp-Aware Metric Data Exports
  • CSV & JSON Export API: `/api/export/csv` and `/api/export/json`.
  • Columns: track_id, class, fine_grained_class, timestamp, frame_index, world_x, world_y,
    velocity_mps, velocity_kmh, acceleration_mps2, heading, distance_travelled_meters, quality_flag.


3. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & API OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Backend Components:
  ├── backend/perception/detection/   -> FastSAHIDetector (Batched PyTorch GPU SAHI)
  ├── backend/perception/tracking/    -> BoTSORTTracker, ByteTrackTracker, SAHIBoTSORTTracker
  ├── backend/perception/classification/ -> FineGrainedClassifier (13 classes + temporal cache)
  ├── backend/telemetry/srt_parser.py -> DJISRTParser (Subtitle telemetry parser & 3D ray projector)
  ├── backend/telemetry/srt_provider.py -> SRTTelemetryProvider (Flight telemetry & auto homography)
  ├── backend/trajectories/homography.py -> RoadPlaneHomography (OpenCV 4-point & optical transform)
  ├── backend/trajectories/speed_estimator.py -> GroundPlaneSpeedEstimator (m/s, km/h, m/s²)
  ├── backend/trajectories/quality.py -> KinematicQualityAssessor (Quality control flagging)
  ├── backend/trajectories/engine.py  -> TrajectoryEngine (Stateful kinematics + outlier gating)
  ├── backend/trajectories/storage.py -> TrajectoryStorage (SQLite persistence + CSV/JSON exporter)
  ├── backend/analytics/engine.py     -> TrafficAnalyticsEngine (Category speeds + distribution)
  └── backend/pipeline.py             -> HeimdallPipeline (Orchestrator + HUD annotation renderer)

Frontend Modules:
  ├── frontend/index.html             -> Command Center UI, Calibration Modal, Inspector & HUD
  ├── frontend/map.js                 -> 2D Canvas Multi-layer Visualizer & Calibration point picker
  ├── frontend/app.js                 -> WebSocket handler, table renderer, and calibration workflow
  └── frontend/style.css              -> Tactical Dark Military Command Center theme

REST Endpoints:
  • GET    /api/trajectories          -> Retrieves all active and persistent trails
  • DELETE /api/trajectories          -> Flushes SQLite database and in-memory trajectory history
  • GET    /api/calibration           -> Retrieves ground-plane homography & SRT telemetry status
  • POST   /api/calibration           -> Calibrates homography from 4 image points and dimensions
  • DELETE /api/calibration           -> Clears calibration and reverts to relative kinematics
  • GET    /api/export/csv            -> Streams full metric observation dataset as CSV
  • GET    /api/export/json           -> Streams full structured trajectory dataset as JSON
  • GET    /api/analytics/summary     -> Aggregates fine-grained distributions and speed spectra


4. RUNNING & VERIFYING THE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Start the server:
     .\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m backend.main

2. Open the Command Center in your browser:
     http://localhost:8000/

3. Run Video Analytics:
     Click "Process Video" -> Select video file (e.g. `Multi_Road_Merged_convert_4k.mp4`) ->
     The system automatically pairs `Multi_Road_1080p.srt` and calculates optical ground homography.

4. Inspect Object-Level Insights:
     Click any tracked road user on the 2D canvas or in the table to view its fine-grained class,
     current km/h velocity, acceleration, world coordinates, distance, and quality status.

5. Download Metric Observations:
     Click "📥 CSV" or "📥 JSON" in the top navigation bar to download full kinematic exports.
