1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While Level 1 established aerial detection and tracking, and Level 2 established
object-level kinematics and fine-grained taxonomy, Level 3 elevates the platform
into an enterprise-grade Aggregate Traffic Intelligence and Macro Analytics Engine.

Level 3 solves the fundamental challenge of converting millions of continuous microscopic
spatio-temporal coordinate points into actionable macro-level operational intelligence for
traffic management centers, city planners, and autonomous drone operations.

Key Innovations & Differentiators in Level 3:
  1. 100% Real-World Derived Analytics: Zero synthetic or hardcoded random metrics. All
     operational KPIs and visualizations are computed directly from the accumulated Level 2
     persistent trajectories, homography ground coordinates, and temporal observation buffers.
  2. Top 6 Operational KPIs: Real-time calculation of Total Flow Rate (veh/min), Space-Mean
     Average Speed (km/h), Traffic Density (veh/km), Road Surface Occupancy (%), Active Queue
     Length (meters), and Peak Flow Rate (veh/min).
  3. 8 Specialized Visual Analytics Modules: High-density interactive visualization suite
     covering time-binned modal flow curves, 12-movement vector intersection diagrams, lane
     corridor passage volumes, modal split ratios, queue evolution time-series, Origin-Destination
     (4x4) spatial matrices, fundamental flow-density diagrams, and spatial speed heatmaps.
  4. Dual Rightmost Interface (Visual & Tabular): High-density tabbed sidebar providing both
     visual graphical widgets (canvas area charts, SVG vector arrows, donuts) and structured
     monospace tabular breakdowns (KPI summary, 12-movement breakdown, lane corridors, OD counts).
  5. Interactive Adjustable Workspace Layout: IDE-like multi-panel horizontal and vertical drag
     splitters with real-time ResizeObserver dynamic canvas scaling and persistent browser
     localStorage memory.
  6. Bi-Directional Cross-Filtering: Seamless cross-filtering where clicking any movement vector,
     lane corridor, or OD cell filters the entire dashboard and highlights matching trajectory
     trails on the 2D spatial canvas in real time.

2. CORE LEVEL 3 ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS
================================================================================

2.1 Top 6 Operational KPIs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Total Traffic Flow Rate (Q):
   - Defined as the total vehicle throughput per unit of time across the observed roadway.
   - Formula:
         Q = N_vehicles / Δt_min  [vehicles / minute]
         Q_hourly = Q * 60        [vehicles / hour]
   - Uses standardized physical time window normalization (Δt_min >= 1.0 min) to ensure
     physically stable, realistic urban flow rates (e.g. 15–60 veh/min, or 900–3600 veh/hr).

2. Space-Mean Average Speed (v_s):
   - Calculated across all confirmed road user trajectories in the selected temporal window.
   - Formula:
         v_s = (1 / N) * Σ (v_i)  [km/h]
   - Consumes true ground-plane homography calibrated speeds (km/h) derived from real ground
     displacement Δs / Δt.

3. Traffic Density (k):
   - Measures spatial concentration of vehicles per kilometer of roadway corridor.
   - Formula:
         k = N_active / L_road_km  [vehicles / kilometer]
   - Computed using active visible vehicle tracks over the calibrated 150-meter roadway footprint.

4. Road Surface Occupancy (ρ):
   - Ratio of total physical ground area occupied by vehicle footprints relative to available
     roadway surface area.
   - Category-Specific Footprints:
         - Heavy Goods (HGV / Buses) : 35.0 m²
         - Passenger Cars / LGV      : 12.5 m²
         - Motorcycles / Scooters    : 3.5 m²
         - Pedestrians / Persons     : 1.5 m²
   - Formula:
         ρ = (Σ Footprint_i / Road_Area_m²) * 100%  [% surface occupied]

5. Active Queue Length (L_q):
   - Tracks the physical length of queued vehicles stopped or crawling at red signals / congestion.
   - Qualification Threshold: Ground speed v < 6.0 km/h with persistent spatial presence.
   - Formula:
         L_q = N_queued * 6.5 meters  [meters]
         (Accounting for 4.5m average vehicle length + 2.0m inter-vehicle safety gap).

6. Peak Flow Rate (Q_peak):
   - Identifies the maximum instantaneous vehicle surge recorded across sliding 10s and 30s bins.
   - Annotated directly on the flow timeline curve as an operational peak indicator.


2.2 The 8 Specialized Visual Analytics Modules
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module 1: Traffic Flow Timeline (Vehicles / Minute)
  • Dynamic time-binned area chart tracking flow volume over the observation session.
  • Stacked modal category breakdown: Cars (Cyan), Motorcycles (Lime), Buses (Purple),
    Heavy Trucks (Crimson), and Other (Slate).
  • Automatic bin width scaling (5s, 10s, 30s) with glowing peak point annotation.

Module 2: 12-Directional Intersection Movement Flow
  • Visualizes all 12 standard intersection movements in an interactive SVG geometry:
        Through Movements : N → S, S → N, E → W, W → E
        Right Turns       : N → W, S → E, E → N, W → S
        Left Turns        : N → E, S → W, E → S, W → N
  • Directional vector Bézier paths with glowing arrowheads (marker-end) and stroke widths
    proportional to observed volume counts.
  • Interactive hover tooltips and click-to-filter cross-filtering.

Module 3: Lane & Corridor Passage Volume Breakdown
  • Classifies trajectories into directional approach corridors:
        - LANE 01: Eastbound North Corridor
        - LANE 02: Westbound South Corridor
        - LANE 03: Southbound West Corridor
        - LANE 04: Northbound East Corridor
  • Displays total passage volume, flow rate (vpm), and modal split progress bars.

Module 4: Modal Split & Vehicle Class Ratio
  • Multi-class donut chart and tabular ratio breakdown computed from the 13-class taxonomy.
  • Displays volume count and percentage share for Cars, Motorcycles, Buses, Trucks, and VRUs.

Module 5: Queue Evolution & Dissipation Time-Series
  • Continuous curve tracking queue length in meters over time.
  • Measures queuing build-up cycles during red signal intervals and rapid dissipation during
    green signal phases.

Module 6: Origin-Destination (OD) 4x4 Spatial Matrix
  • Complete 4x4 spatial passage matrix mapping Origin approach to Destination exit (N, S, E, W).
  • Color-intensity background heatmap with diagonal through-flow suppression and cell click
    filtering.

Module 7: Flow-Density Fundamental Diagram (Greenshields Traffic Theory)
  • Empirical scatter plot mapping observed flow rate against spatial traffic density Q(k).
  • Classifies current traffic regime into three operational states:
        - Free Flow State  (Green)  : Density < 30 veh/km, High speed, unobstructed travel.
        - High Flow State  (Blue)   : Density 30–65 veh/km, Maximum capacity throughput.
        - Congested State  (Red)    : Density > 65 veh/km, Breakdown in speed, queue buildup.

Module 8: Spatial Speed Kinematics Heatmap
  • Real-time 2D spatial canvas layer mapping trajectory speed gradients directly over aerial footage.
  • Color scale: Green (> 40 km/h) -> Yellow (20–40 km/h) -> Orange (6–20 km/h) -> Crimson (< 6 km/h).

3. USER INTERFACE & WORKSPACE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

3.1 Rightmost Vertical Sidebar (Visual & Tabular Tabs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To keep the primary aerial gimbal payload video feed and 2D spatial canvas unobstructed,
the analytics engine is embedded into the rightmost vertical sidebar with three modes:
  1. `[TELEMETRY]`: Live drone flight telemetry, active road users by class, and selected track inspector.
  2. `[L3 VISUAL]`: Time range filter chips (LIVE, 5M, 10M, 30M, ALL), Top 6 KPI strip grid,
     Flow Timeline Canvas, 12-Movement SVG vector diagram, Lane Volume bars, Modal Donut,
     Queue Evolution Canvas, OD Matrix heatmap, and Flow-Density scatter canvas.
  3. `[L3 TABULAR]`: Structured high-density monospace tables for:
     - Operational KPIs Summary (Metric, Value, Unit, Status Badge)
     - 12 Movement Directional Counts (Corridor, Volume, Share %)
     - Lane Corridor Performance (Lane ID, Volume, Flow Rate, Modal Split)
     - Vehicle Taxonomy Distribution (Class, Count, Share %)
     - Origin-Destination Raw Count Matrix

3.2 Full-Width Persistent Track Registry Table
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bottom panel hosts the full-width Live Persistent Track Registry & Kinematics Log:
  • Columns: TRACK ID, CLASS, SUBCLASS, CONFIDENCE, VELOCITY (km/h), ACCEL (m/s²), HEADING, DURATION, QUALITY.
  • Displays real calibrated SI units (km/h and m/s²) without truncation or overflow.

3.3 Interactive Resizable Split Layout (IDE-like Drag Handles)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Horizontal Resizer 1 (`#gutterCol1`): Drag to adjust width ratio between Aerial Video and 2D Map.
• Horizontal Resizer 2 (`#gutterCol2`): Drag to adjust horizontal width of the Right Analytics Sidebar (260px to 850px).
• Vertical Resizer (`#gutterRow`): Drag to adjust height of the bottom Persistent Track Registry Table (70px to 550px).
• Real-time `ResizeObserver` automatically redraws and fits the 2D trajectory canvas during dragging.
• Browser `localStorage` persistence saves customized layout dimensions across page reloads.
