1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Level 4 answers the ultimate operational question:
"WHERE EXACTLY IS EACH TRAFFIC EVENT HAPPENING ON THE REAL ROAD NETWORK?"

By coupling the microscopic detections (Level 1), real-world kinematics (Level 2),
and macro aggregate analytics (Level 3) with synchronized DJI flight telemetry and
topological road network models, Level 4 transforms aerial image coordinates into
geographically grounded, map-native urban traffic intelligence.

Core Innovations in Level 4:
  1. Telemetry-Driven 3D Ray-to-Ground Georeferencer: Translates image pixel footprints
     (u, v) into metric ground offsets (X, Y) relative to the drone and converts them to
     true WGS-84 coordinates (Latitude, Longitude) using DJI SRT metadata (altitude AGL,
     gimbal pitch/yaw, focal length, sensor optical geometry).
  2. Directional Map-Matching Engine: Topologically matches vehicle trajectories to physical
     road segments, approach corridors, and individual lanes while enforcing directional
     heading continuity (cos(Δθ) > 0.25) to prevent cross-carriageway jumping.
  3. Real-World Geographic Road Map (Leaflet.js): Integrated dark aerospace CartoDB/OSM
     vector map layer displaying road segments, junction boundaries, physical lanes, and
     real-time grounded vehicle markers with headings and GPS trail polylines.
  4. Geographic Desire Lines: Visualizes 12 origin → destination movement corridors routed
     over the actual physical road layout with line thicknesses scaled by vehicle volume.
  5. Per-Lane Spatial Metrics: Computes volume, flow rate (vpm), space-mean speed (km/h),
     density (vpk), surface occupancy (%), and active queue length for each physical lane.
  6. Physical Spatial Queue Extents: Renders actual queue bars along the carriageway with
     precise start GPS and end GPS coordinates, queue length in meters, and dissipation state.
  7. Bidirectional Video ↔ Map Synchronization: Selecting any vehicle in the aerial video
     highlights its GPS position and road corridor on the map, and selecting any map marker
     locates the vehicle bounding box in the video feed.
  8. Georeferenced Exporters: Complete streaming support for standard GeoJSON FeatureCollections
     and georeferenced spatial CSV datasets.

2. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & SPATIAL PIPELINE
================================================================================

2.1 Camera-to-Ground 3D Ray Intersection & WGS-84 Conversion
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Step 1: Camera Ray Vector in Optical Frame:
      x_c = (u - c_x) / f_x
      y_c = (v - c_y) / f_y
      z_c = 1.0
      v_c = [x_c, y_c, z_c]^T / ||[x_c, y_c, z_c]||

• Step 2: 3D Rotation to World Metric Frame:
      R_c2w = R_yaw(ψ) * R_pitch(θ) * R_roll(φ)
      v_w = R_c2w * v_c

• Step 3: Ground-Plane Ray Intersection (Ground Z = 0, Drone at [0, 0, H_AGL]):
      s = -H_AGL / v_{w, z}
      X_m = s * v_{w, x}  [East meters from drone nadir]
      Y_m = s * v_{w, y}  [North meters from drone nadir]

• Step 4: Geodetic WGS-84 Local Tangent Plane Projection:
      m_lat = 111132.954 - 559.822 * cos(2φ) + 1.175 * cos(4φ)  [meters/deg lat]
      m_lon = (π * a_wgs84 * cos(φ)) / (180 * sqrt(1 - e² * sin²(φ)))  [meters/deg lon]
      
      Latitude_veh  = Latitude_drone + (Y_m / m_lat)
      Longitude_veh = Longitude_drone + (X_m / m_lon)

• Zero Fabrication Policy:
      Every georeferenced coordinate is tagged with an explicit confidence flag:
        - `HIGH_CONFIDENCE (CALIBRATED)`
        - `MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE (TELEMETRY_ESTIMATED)`
        - `LOW_CONFIDENCE (UNCALIBRATED)`


2.2 Map-Matching & Directional Continuity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Orthogonal Cross-Track Distance (d_perp):
      Measures perpendicular distance in meters from vehicle GPS coordinate to candidate
      road segment polyline AB.

• Longitudinal Stationing (s_parallel):
      Measures linear distance in meters along the road segment from the upstream origin.

• Directional Heading Alignment Scoring:
      align_score = max(0.0, cos(radians(θ_veh - θ_segment)))
      If align_score < 0.25 (vehicle travelling opposite to one-way or dual carriageway),
      a heavy penalty (+40.0m) is applied to discard the wrong-way corridor.

• Lane Assignment:
      Selects the nearest physical lane centerline within the matched segment.


2.3 Geographic Desire Lines
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• For each of the 12 intersection movements (e.g. N → S, N → E, S → W, E → W):
      - Line width = max(1.5, min(8.0, 1.5 + (V_movement / V_max) * 6.5))  [pixels]
      - Routed directly through the junction polygon connecting physical corridor nodes.
      - Displays volume count and modal percentage on hover.


2.4 Per-Lane Spatial Metrics & Queues
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Lane Flow Rate (Q_lane)       : Q = V_lane / Δt_min  [veh/min]
• Space-Mean Speed (v_avg)      : v_avg = (1 / N) * Σ (v_i)  [km/h]
• Lane Traffic Density (k_lane) : k = N_active / L_lane_km  [veh/km]
• Surface Occupancy (ρ_lane)    : ρ = (Σ Footprint_i / (L_lane * W_lane)) * 100%  [%]
• Physical Queue Extents        :
      - Qualified by ground speed v < 6.0 km/h with persistent spatial presence.
      - Start GPS : Intersection stop bar coordinate [lon, lat].
      - End GPS   : Linearly interpolated queue tail [lon, lat] along approach polyline.
      - Queue Length = N_queued * 6.5 meters.
